# Timeline (verified items link to source)
- 2013: scholarship regulation (Decision 58/2013).
- 2019: ~2,634 struggling overseas students, 2013/14–2017/18 (Audit Bureau, via Al-Jarida) — https://www.aljarida.com/article/68482
- 2020–2022: 4,432 students dismissed/withdrawn/cancelled/frozen; most withdrawals "voluntary" (54% 2020, 78% 2021, 77% 2022).
- 2023: Audit Bureau report names freezing as a main cause of termination → stricter freeze rules — https://kuwaittimes.com/article/22632/kuwait/other-news/kuwait-enforces-stricter-regulations-on-freezing-government-scholarships/
- 2025-03: Ministry amends overseas scholarship regulation for 2025/26 — https://www.alanba.com.kw/1298737
- 2025-07-17: Ministerial Decision 197/2025, new scholarship regulation.
- 2025/26: grievance window opened for the 2025–26 plan — https://www.aljarida.com/article/102705
- 2026/27: plan announced; accepted students must sign financial pledge within 60 days or scholarship is ended — https://www.mohe.edu.kw/site/ar/eServices/ServiceDetails/1?PageID=3
